Cedar Grove Greenhouse Maintenance Guide

Daily Irrigation
The automated irrigation cycle begins at 5:30 a.m. each day. Staff must inspect the main lines before the cycle starts and record any visible leak in the maintenance log.

Climate Targets
During normal production, relative humidity should remain between 60% and 70%. Ventilation fans start automatically when humidity exceeds the upper limit for ten continuous minutes. Staff should not override the fans unless a supervisor authorizes it.

Sensor Calibration
Temperature and humidity sensors are calibrated every Monday morning. The reference instrument is stored in the locked cabinet beside the control room. A sensor that differs from the reference by more than two percentage points must be replaced or recalibrated.

Nutrient System
The primary nutrient tank is identified as GREEN-4. Its level is checked before every irrigation cycle. Concentrate must never be poured directly into an active irrigation line.

Plant Intake
New plants remain in the intake area for a 48-hour observation period before joining the production rows. Staff inspect the leaves, soil, and containers for signs of pests during this period.

Emergency Shutdown
The red switch beside the control panel stops pumps, heaters, and ventilation equipment. After an emergency shutdown, only the maintenance supervisor may restart the automated system.
