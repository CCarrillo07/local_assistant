Atlas Distribution Center Safety Manual

Traffic Rules
The maximum forklift speed inside the warehouse is 8 kilometers per hour. Forklifts must yield to pedestrians at every marked crossing. Operators must sound the horn before entering a blind intersection.

Zone Markings
Blue floor markings identify pedestrian walkways. Yellow markings identify equipment lanes. Red markings indicate areas that must remain clear for emergency access and fire equipment.

Personal Protective Equipment
Safety shoes and high-visibility vests are required on the warehouse floor. Eye protection is also required in the battery-charging and maintenance areas. Visitors must remain with an authorized escort.

Incident Reporting
Injuries, collisions, spills, and damaged storage racks must be reported to the shift supervisor within 30 minutes. The affected area must be isolated until a trained employee confirms that operations can resume.

Emergency Evacuation
When the evacuation alarm sounds, employees must stop equipment safely and leave through the nearest marked exit. The primary evacuation point is the Gate C assembly area. Supervisors perform a head count and report missing personnel to the emergency coordinator.

Daily Inspection
Forklift operators complete a documented inspection before the first use of each shift. A vehicle with a failed brake, steering, warning-light, or horn check must be removed from service.
