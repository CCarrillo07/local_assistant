Meridian Labs Employee Leave Policy

Eligibility
Full-time employees become eligible for paid leave after completing 90 calendar days of employment. Contractors and temporary staff follow the terms of their individual agreements.

Annual Allowance
Eligible employees receive 18 paid leave days during each calendar year. Leave is credited monthly and appears in the employee portal at the beginning of each month.

Requests
Planned leave requests should be submitted at least five business days before the first day of absence. The employee's manager approves requests after considering staffing and project commitments. Emergency leave may be reported on the same day by contacting the manager directly.

Carryover
Employees may carry over up to five unused days into the following calendar year. Carried days must be used by March 31. Any remaining carried balance expires after that date and is not converted into cash.

Illness
An employee who is absent for three or more consecutive workdays because of illness must provide supporting documentation to Human Resources. Medical details should not be sent through ordinary team chat.

Separation
Unused leave is handled according to the employment agreement and applicable local law. Employees should contact Human Resources for a calculation before their final working day.
